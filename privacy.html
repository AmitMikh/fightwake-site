Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

FightWake is an Android app that wakes you up for MMA fights. This page explains what it collects and why. There are no accounts and no ads.

What we store

When you first open the app, it registers your install with the FightWake server and gets a random device id. For each install the server stores:

  • A salted hash of your phone's Android ID. We never store the ID itself.
  • The Firebase Cloud Messaging token used to send you alarms and notifications.
  • The language you picked in the app.
  • The alarms and notifications you set.
  • Whether you have Pro or an Event Pass.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. FightWake never sees your payment details. RevenueCat receives the random device id, not your Android ID.

Push messages

Alarms and notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service.

Security and deletion

Everything is sent over HTTPS. Uninstalling the app stops all collection. To have your install's data deleted, {{contact}} and we'll remove it.

Children

FightWake is meant for adults and isn't designed for children.
